# Gestalt Grove — Implementation Roadmap

## Purpose

Gestalt Grove's foundational community design is documented. The current work is to gather the people and resources needed to realize it, then translate that design into a functioning village.

This roadmap describes **implementation stages and prerequisites**, not a fixed schedule or an invitation to redesign the community. Land, funding, site-specific engineering, construction sequencing, and implementation details remain dependent on circumstances and the people involved.

The [Comprehensive Design](comprehensive-design/GestaltGrove_ComprehensiveDesign.md) remains the authoritative specification. The modular documents are introductions to its systems, not competing proposals.

---

## Guiding Principles

- Preserve the ethical foundation and core design decisions.
- Build relationships among prospective residents, collaborators, and implementation partners.
- Choose practical steps in response to real resources, opportunities, and site conditions.
- Document what is implemented and correct transcription or documentation errors without reopening settled foundational decisions.
- Allow implementation details to be determined when enough information exists to make them responsibly.

No acreage, particular parcel, or acquisition method is a fixed prerequisite to the project.

---

## Stage 0 — Community Design and Documentation

**Status: Completed as the foundation for implementation.**

The integrated design, ethical principles, governance model, economic concepts, community-life arrangements, and intended infrastructure have been documented publicly.

This does not mean every technical implementation choice has already been made. It means the project is no longer seeking open-ended proposals to redefine its foundational vision. Documentation corrections and clarifications remain welcome when they restore fidelity to the intended design. New ideas are always welcome.

---

## Stage 1 — Community Formation and Implementation Partnerships

**Status: Current focus.**

The immediate priority is to connect with people who understand Gestalt Grove and may wish to help build, operate, support, or eventually live in it.

Activities may include:
- sharing the community's documented vision through accessible introductions and explanations
- connecting with prospective residents, artists, engineers, builders, educators, and other collaborators
- learning which skills, interests, and practical resources prospective participants can offer
- building trust and identifying suitable implementation partnerships
- organizing early digital community spaces and communications tools

Expressions of interest are exploratory; they do not guarantee residency, a role, or a timetable.

---

## Stage 2 — Land, Funding, and Site Preparation

**Status: Opportunities and prerequisites under development.**

Acquire access to suitable land through whichever arrangement proves viable, and organize the resources required for responsible initial development.

Activities will depend on the site and may include:
- comparing land opportunities and acquisition arrangements
- determining the appropriate site layout and achievable number of structures
- preparing legal, financial, permitting, utility, and construction arrangements
- completing site-specific surveys and engineering work
- sequencing earthworks, water management, food-production infrastructure, energy systems, and essential services

The community's intended systems remain consistent with the Comprehensive Design, while dimensions, placement, construction order, and other site-dependent details respond to the land actually obtained.

### Passive Water-Harvesting Earthworks

Site preparation will incorporate passive, hybrid water-harvesting earthworks, including back-sloping terraces, hügelkultur trenches, and hügel-swales, to capture runoff, retain moisture, and support the outdoor multi-layered food forest.

The approach is documented in greater detail in [Site Preparation — Passive Water-Harvesting Earthworks](site-planning/WaterHarvestingEarthworks.md), including the original 15-acre reference scenario and its adaptation to other potential sites.

---

## Stage 3 — Essential Infrastructure and Initial Construction

Begin constructing the systems that support safe habitation and continued development, according to site-specific needs and resources.

Activities will include:
- earthworks and water-harvesting infrastructure
- utilities, power, sanitation, and communications
- early food-growing systems and greenhouses
- initial residential Dhomes and shared-use facilities
- deployment of practical village operations, task coordination, and inspection workflows

Each residential Dhome will be designed for its intended household and will include an attached climate-controlled aquaponics greenhouse. Construction counts and configurations depend on available land and resources.

---

## Stage 4 — Initial Residency and Village Operations

Welcome residents as the necessary facilities and operational systems become ready.

Priorities include:
- establishing the six-hour compensated weekly task system and rotating task inspections
- implementing the weekly Wednesday meal-meetings
- operating the separate residency stipend and task-payment mechanisms
- activating shared food, water, energy, cultural, and creative facilities as they become available
- resolving implementation details and operational issues through the community's ethical governance processes

The village need not reach its ideal scale before the first residents can begin living there.

---

## Stage 5 — Continued Buildout, Learning, and Knowledge Sharing

Continue toward the intended village design as resources allow. Record practical experience, maintain the documentation, and share useful knowledge with others building compatible communities.

As the village develops, practical implementation details may be refined in light of operating experience. This is different from reopening Gestalt Grove's foundational design for wholesale reconsideration.

There is no required expansion timetable or fixed number of residential structures independent of the land available.

---

## How to Participate Now

The immediate invitation is to **help realize Gestalt Grove's documented design**. People who identify with its principles can explore the [Comprehensive Design](comprehensive-design/GestaltGrove_ComprehensiveDesign.m), learn about expected resident life and responsibilities, and express interest in relevant construction, technical, creative, financial, logistical, or community-building work.

Useful contributions include concrete implementation help, relevant experience, connections to resources or opportunities, and precise reports of documentation mistakes.

For participation guidance, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [CALL.md](CALL.md).

---

## What This Roadmap Avoids

- artificial urgency
- speculative promises
- growth-driven decision-making
- dependency on saviors, investors, or founders
- narratives that require perfection to succeed

Gestalt Grove does not rush.

It endures.

---

## Final Note

This roadmap exists to provide orientation.

If you are reading this and wondering whether you are “late” or “early”:

You are neither.

You are here when the system is still being implemented, which is exactly when careful people are needed most.
