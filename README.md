**Gestalt Grove is a systems-designed ecovillage in the process of being built.**

It is not a commune, a retreat, or an experiment in idealism.
It is an operating system for ethical, creative, sustainable living - designed deliberately, documented publicly, and built with people who understand that better systems don’t appear on their own.

---

## THE CALL

**This village is not an idea.
It is a decision.**

Most attempts at intentional community fail for predictable reasons.

They optimize for comfort instead of ethics.
For consensus instead of clarity.
For ideals instead of systems.

What they build feels good... until it doesn’t.

**Gestalt Grove** is not an escape from the world. 
It is an **operating system for a way of life** - one designed to make creativity, ethics, sustainability, and autonomy *structurally inevitable*, not aspirational.

---

## THE PROBLEM

The modern world burns creativity for fuel.

People work to survive instead of to build.
Communities form around vibes instead of values.
Power accumulates where ethics are optional.
And most “alternatives” quietly reproduce the same failures they claim to reject.

We are told to choose between:
* freedom *or* stability
* individuality *or* cooperation
* technology *or* nature

That is a false trilemma... and we are done accepting it.

---

## THE CLAIM

A different way of living is not only possible...
it is **engineerable**.

If ethics are made foundational rather than optional,
if work is contribution rather than coercion,
if food, water, energy, and shelter are treated as infrastructure instead of profit centers,
and if art and engineering are recognized as *essential systems*, not luxuries...

then a village can function like a healthy organism instead of a fragile social experiment.

**This is what we are building.**

---

## WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT

**Ethics are executable**
- Every system is designed to increase creativity without diminishing it elsewhere. Unethical shortcuts are structurally disallowed, not politely discouraged.

**No one pays to exist**
- Housing is not rented. Life is not monetized. Contribution replaces rent.

**Work is limited, meaningful, and transparent**
- Six  compensated hours a week sustains the whole. Everything beyond that is choice - and also compensated.

**Community-directed decision-making** 
- Every proposal begins with a `Yes`, `No`, or `No Preference` ballot. Unanimous decisions are resolved immediately, objections initiate consensus discussions, and proposals without opposition may proceed through appropriate additional voting methods. The process remains subject to Gestlat Grove's ethical foundation.

**Art and engineering are core infrastructure**
- Not hobbies. Not branding. Not side projects. The village is built *around* them.

**Technology serves autonomy**
- Not surveillance. Not extraction. Not dependence. Tools are chosen to decentralize power, not concentrate it.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### **Is this utopian?**

No. Utopias collapse because they deny human complexity.

This project assumes:
* disagreement will occur
* mistakes will be made
* systems must be designed to absorb friction without breaking

Resilience, not perfection, is the goal.

### **How is work handled?**

Each resident completes six compensated hours of village-supporting work weekly, including rotating task-inspection duties when scheduled. Additional work is voluntary and compensated separately.

No one works to survive.
No one is forced to “do their passion.”
Transparency replaces hustle.

### **Who decides what happens?**

The people who live there, using systems designed to prevent domination, coercion, and stagnation.

Consensus is real consensus, not majority rule with better branding.
Objections require imagination and proposals for possible solutions.

### **How can I get involved now?**

Right now, involvement looks like:
* understanding the documented vision and resident responsibilities
* signaling interest as a future resident, collaborator, or builder
* offering relevant skills, practical resources, or implementation partnerships
* reporting specific documentation errors or inconsistencies
