# Gestalt Grove: Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct exists to protect ethical collaboration, creative autonomy, and constructive engagement within the Gestalt Grove project.

It applies to all spaces associated with this repository, including:
- issues
- pull requests
- discussions
- external platforms where Gestalt Grove work is represented

This document derives directly from `ETHICS.md`.

---

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:
- engage respectfully and in good faith
- communicate clearly and honestly
- offer constructive feedback
- accept correction without defensiveness
- respect personal boundaries and autonomy
- prioritize ethical integrity over winning arguments

Disagreement is normal.
Manipulation is not.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:
- harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- coercion, manipulation, or pressure tactics
- domination of discussion through volume or repetition
- bad-faith argumentation or deliberate misrepresentation
- ideological evangelism unrelated to project alignment
- attempts to bypass ethical or governance constraints

Intent does not override impact.

---

## Ethical Boundaries

Participants may not:
- propose systems that rely on coercion, exploitation, or harm
- advocate for “ends justify means” reasoning
- dismiss ethical constraints as impractical or optional
- demand compliance through majority pressure

Ethics are not negotiable.

---

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
- requests for correction or clarification
- temporary restriction of participation
- removal from discussions or contribution channels

Enforcement actions are:
- proportional
- documented
- focused on preventing harm rather than on punishment

Repeated unethical behavior indicates misalignment.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or observe conduct that violates this Code:
- raise the issue calmly and directly when possible
- otherwise, report it through appropriate project channels

Concerns will be handled discreetly and ethically.

---

## Final Note

Gestalt Grove is not a space for domination, spectacle, or ideological conquest.

It is a space for careful thinking, honest collaboration, and building systems that remain ethical under pressure.

Participation here is a choice.
Ethical conduct is a requirement.
