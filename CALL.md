### **CALL TO THE VILLAGERS**

**Gestalt Grove is being built.
Not someday... deliberately.**

This is not a commune.
Not a retreat.
Not an experiment in idealism.

Gestalt Grove is a **systems-designed ecovillage**, an operating system for ethical, creative, sustainable living, built for people who understand that better ways of living don’t emerge by accident.

Most intentional communities fail for predictable reasons.
They optimize for comfort instead of ethics.
For consensus instead of clarity.
For ideals instead of systems.

Gestalt Grove is designed to survive those failures.

It is built on:
* explicit, non-negotiable ethics
* governance that prevents power accumulation
* an economy based on contribution instead of extraction
* infrastructure designed for resilience
* daily life that protects autonomy and creativity

This project is **pre-land, pre-capital, post-decision**.

What exists already:
* a coherent ethical foundation
* a complete governance and economic framework
* clear documentation, shared publicly
* a commitment to build without coercion or urgency

We are looking for people who:
* care more about integrity than aesthetics
* are willing to think in systems
* understand that patience is a form of competence
* want to help shape something durable from a documented design

There is no residency application to complete at this early stage. The **Interest & Alignment Census** is an optional way to understand who resonates with this project, what skills and perspectives are present, and what conditions people need to participate meaningfully; completing it is not a residency commitment or guarantee. It simply helps us design responsibly. The comprehensive village design describes a separate audition/selection process for prospective residents when that stage becomes appropriate.

If this call changed your pulse even slightly,
you’re invited to take the next step.

👉 **[Contribute your perspective via the Gestalt Grove Census](https://tally.so/r/RGD1Yp)**

You are not early.
You are here as the documented vision moves toward implementation, which is exactly when careful people matter most.
