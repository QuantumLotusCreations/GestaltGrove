# Site Preparation — Passive Water-Harvesting Earthworks

## Design Approach

Gestalt Grove's initial site preparation will incorporate passive, hybrid water-harvesting earthworks intended to capture and retain precipitation, slow surface runoff, reduce erosion, and establish favorable growing conditions for the outdoor multi-layered food forest.

The final layout, dimensions, and distribution of these earthworks will depend on the actual land acquired.

## 15-Acre Reference Scenario

The following describes the site-preparation approach developed for a 15-acre scenario. The acreage figures are specific to that scenario; the underlying water-harvesting strategy can be adapted to different property sizes and configurations.

> Initial site preparation will focus entirely on passive, hybrid water-harvesting earthworks across 10 of the initial 15 acres to capture potential flash-flooding and runoff from anticipated heavy Super El Niño precipitation cycles. Instead of managing this influx as a weather hazard, the landscape will be sculpted with earthen back-sloping terraces, sunken hügelkultur trenches, and hügel-swales. Rainfall and snowmelt will drain backward into three-foot-deep subterranean "wood sponges," slowing runoff, reducing erosion, extending moisture retention across seasons, and establishing the hydrological foundation for the outdoor multi-layered food forest.

## Adapting the Approach

The earthwork techniques, landscape integration, and water-retention objectives will inform planning for the eventual site. Specific acreage allocations, terrace dimensions, trench placement, and construction sequencing will be adapted to the available land.
