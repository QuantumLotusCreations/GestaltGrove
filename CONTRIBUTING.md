# Contributing to Gestalt Grove

Gestalt Grove's foundational community design is documented, and the project is moving toward community formation and physical implementation. Contributions are welcome from people who want to **help bring that design into reality**.

This repository is a public reference and communication resource. Its modular documents introduce portions of the [Comprehensive Design](comprehensive-design/GestaltGrove_ComprehensiveDesign.md), which remains authoritative unless a specific detail is explicitly revised.

---

## Before You Contribute

Please become familiar with:
- The [Comprehensive Design](comprehensive-design/GestaltGrove_ComprehensiveDesign.md) *This is the authoritative integrated design; modular documents do not supersede its details unless an explicit revision is declared.*
- [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md)
- [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)
- [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)

The aim is to understand what Gestalt Grove is being built to become, including the resident responsibilities and ethical commitments involved.

---

## Ways to Help

The project welcomes contact from prospective residents and people interested in contributing practical skills, work, resources, or partnerships to implementation. Relevant areas may include building, land and infrastructure preparation, sustainable food systems, engineering, software, art, education, logistics, funding, and communications.

For repository-specific contributions, useful help includes:
- identifying factual inconsistencies, transcription errors, broken links, and missing citations
- correcting modular summaries that misrepresent the Comprehensive Design
- improving readability or navigation **without changing the underlying decisions**
- preparing implementation documentation for a specific, agreed task

Where an implementation detail has not yet been selected, for example, the exact voting method for a type of routine decision, specific options can be explored **when that work is needed**. This does not reopen the foundational community design.

---

## What This Repository Is Not Soliciting

- open-ended critiques intended to replace the community's foundational vision
- wholesale redesign proposals or alternative governance philosophies
- changes that weaken its ethical commitments
- speculative rewrites presented as established design decisions

Questions seeking to understand the design are welcome. Corrections that restore its intended meaning are welcome. A suggestion is not automatically an invitation to reopen a settled decision.

---

## How to Contribute

For documentation corrections, open a GitHub issue describing the exact file and passage, the discrepancy, and the relevant Comprehensive Design reference. Small pull requests that implement an identified correction are useful; substantial rewrites should be discussed before being submitted.

For prospective residency, collaboration, or implementation support, use the current contact channels listed in the [README](README.md) or [CALL](CALL.md).

Please follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and engage respectfully and precisely.

---

## Final Note

Gestalt Grove is seeking people to help **build and inhabit** an already articulated community, while doing the practical work necessary to make it real. The design's public availability also allows others to study and utilize its concepts for their own independent projects.
