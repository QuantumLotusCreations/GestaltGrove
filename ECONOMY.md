# Gestalt Grove: Economy

## Purpose

The economy of Gestalt Grove exists to:
- ensure material needs are met without coercion
- reward contribution without creating dependency
- prevent extraction, hoarding, or domination
- support creativity, autonomy, and long-term sustainability

The economy is a **tool**, not a value system.
Ethics remain primary.

---

## Foundational Principles

1. No one pays to exist.  
2. No one is forced to monetize their creativity.  
3. Contribution replaces rent.  
4. Compensation replaces obligation.  
5. Transparency replaces trust-by-assumption.  

Economic systems must remain subordinate to ethics and governance.

---

## Contribution

### Baseline Contribution

Each resident completes six hours of compensated community-supporting tasks per week to sustain shared infrastructure and communal systems. Rotating task inspections count toward those hours and are compensated as work, subject to the task-payment process.

This contribution:
- is limited in scope and duration
- is not monetized externally
- is designed to be predictable and humane

Baseline contribution exists to:
- keep the village functional
- prevent invisible labor
- avoid burnout and resentment

No one contributes indefinitely without rest or recognition.

### Voluntary Contribution

Beyond the baseline:
- all work is voluntary
- all work is compensated
- participation is opt-in

No one is pressured to “turn passion into productivity.”

## Compensation

Circuitree is the village's proposed currency, not the token used to cast governance votes. The governance system has its own voting mechanism; the two systems may interoperate, such as awarding Circuitree for participation in voting.

### Circuitree

Gestalt Grove uses an internal currency, **Circuitree**, to:
- compensate work
- facilitate exchange
- track contribution
- support local circulation

Circuitree is designed to:
- circulate, not accumulate
- reward participation, not ownership
- remain subordinate to real-world needs

It is a *means of coordination*, not a speculative asset.

### Compensation Model

- Every resident receives a separate weekly Circuitree stipend simply for being a resident; it is **not** payment for work and is not conditioned on task completion or inspection.
- Required weekly work is compensated upon satisfactory completion under the task-inspection and payment rules.
- Additional voluntary work is compensated according to applicable rates and completion conditions.
- Rates are transparent and collectively reviewed
- Compensation reflects effort and impact, not status

No one is paid for control.
No one is unpaid for contribution.

### Token Emission Framework
#### Economic Consensus & Automated Distribution Mechanics

The Gestalt Grove economy will be partially sustained by Circuitree, our native cryptocurrency asset. It will operate independently from our voting token to eliminate speculation and wealth hoarding. Circuitree will be automatically minted and distributed based on three distinct cryptographic consensus models that track actual active contribution to the community.

#### Core Token Distribution Matrix

| Consensus Protocol | Triggering Event / Validation Mechanism | Recipient Address Allocation | Main Village Address Match |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Proof of Frequency (PoF)** | Automated Weekly Cycle (Weeks 1–12) | **450 tokens** / week | **+450 tokens** / week |
| **Proof of Frequency (PoF)** | Automated Weekly Cycle (Weeks 13+) | **720 tokens** / week | **+720 tokens** / week |
| **Proof of Participation (PoP)** | Satisfactory Completion of a Task or Job | **Variable Pay Rate** (Set by Voted Category) | **100% Match** of Paid Task Value |
| **Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET)** | Randomized Validation Selection | Opportunities based on long-term engagement | Network Integrity Verification |

#### Protocol Operational Mechanics

##### 1. Proof of Frequency (PoF) - Baseline Stipends
- **The Onboarding Step:** New addresses receive a baseline stipend of **450 tokens per week** during their initial 12 weeks of community integration.
- **The Established Phase:** From week 13 onward, the stipend permanently increases to **720 tokens per week** until an address is explicitly removed by governance.
- **The Multiplier:** To fuel municipal accounts, whenever a PoF distribution triggers, an identical matching sum is automatically minted and sent directly to Gestalt Grove’s main administrative address.

##### 2. Proof of Participation (PoP) - Task Logistics
- **The Trigger:** A cryptographic signal is broadcast across the network the moment a resident successfully completes an assigned 6-hour task or an independent job, verified by an active 3-person inspection group.
- **The Wage Scaling:** Residents receive an hourly baseline of **120 Circuitree** upon completing their core 6 hours. Every consecutive task completed inside that week adds a **+24 Circuitree** bonus to the original base rate. 
- **The Reserve Match:** Just like PoF, the system mints a 100% equivalent match of the paid task value and deposits it into the village's primary address to maintain infrastructure resources.

##### 3. Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET) - Block Validation
- **Equality of Opportunity:** Validation opportunities are assigned via a randomized selection engine that evaluates non-technical contributions, including teaching skills, creating art, or managing community projects. 
- **Consistency Weighting:** Instead of measuring validation eligibility through computing hardware power or financial wealth, eligibility scales relative to a user's consistent, long-term involvement in the village ecosystem over time.

## Needs and Access

Essential needs, including food, water, shelter, and shared infrastructure, are not contingent on wealth accumulation.

Access is governed by:
- participation
- ethical alignment
- shared responsibility

Scarcity is addressed collectively, not competitively.

---

## External Exchange

Gestalt Grove does not isolate itself economically.

The system may:
- interact with external currencies
- engage in ethical trade
- generate revenue through art, education, and services

External income exists to:
- meet external obligations
- reduce internal scarcity
- increase resilience

Profit is a constraint, not a goal.

---

## Transparency and Accountability

All economic systems are:
- documented
- inspectable
- subject to revision

Hidden incentives are treated as system failures.

Economic feedback loops must be visible to remain ethical.

---

## Failure Modes (Explicitly Acknowledged)

This economy is designed to resist:
- unpaid emotional labor
- quiet hierarchy through wealth
- burnout disguised as dedication
- guilt-based participation
- “temporary” emergencies that never end

If these patterns emerge, the system is revised, not defended.

---

## Evolution Without Drift

Economic changes are permitted only if they:
- improve fairness or clarity
- reduce coercion
- increase resilience
- remain aligned with ETHICS.md

Economic complexity may increase.
Economic domination may not.

---

## Final Note

The goal of Gestalt Grove’s economy is not growth.

It is **continuity**:
- people able to live
- systems able to adapt
- creativity able to flourish without fear

An economy that requires exploitation to function is already bankrupt.
